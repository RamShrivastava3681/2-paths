# Two Paths — 12-Slide PowerPoint Prompt

> **How to use:** Copy everything from the divider line below into your presentation AI tool
> (Gamma, Tome, Microsoft Copilot in PowerPoint, Canva Magic Design, or ChatGPT with a deck
> builder). It produces a 12-slide deck that walks through the whole platform — with deep,
> dedicated coverage of Product Catalog, Inventory, and Demand Forecasting.

---

```
Create a 12-slide professional presentation titled "Two Paths — Turn Receivables into Working
Capital", a product walkthrough of the Two Paths platform (a receivables-factoring and debtor-
monitoring SaaS). Audience: prospective clients and investors. Tone: confident, financial,
clean. Visual style: modern fintech — dark navy/white background, electric blue (#0066FF) and
cyan (#00B8FF) accents, generous whitespace, cards, and simple line icons. Use the lucide icon
set. Every slide: a strong headline, 4–6 tight bullets, and a visual suggestion. The deck must
cover EVERY page of the platform, so several slides group related pages — label grouped pages
as "Pages covered" so nothing is missed. Slides 8–10 (Catalog, Inventory, Forecasting) are the
showcase: give them the most visual detail.

────────────────────────────────────────────
SLIDE 1 — TITLE
────────────────────────────────────────────
Headline: "Two Paths — Receivables Factoring & Monitoring Platform"
Subtitle: "Turn outstanding invoices into working capital — without losing sight of risk."
Bullets:
• Institutional-grade invoice factoring combined with real-time debtor monitoring
• Submit, advance, collect — while aging, concentration, and credit risk move live
• Product version badge: v2.4 · Live receivables monitoring
Visual: hero stat strip (₹2.4B advanced, 11 hrs median time to fund, 0.42% loss rate, 98.7%
collection rate), product logo, invoice + shield motif.
Pages covered: (overview — landing page / home)

────────────────────────────────────────────
SLIDE 2 — GETTING IN: LANDING PAGE, AUTH & ROLE-BASED CONSOLES
────────────────────────────────────────────
Headline: "One platform, six job-specific consoles"
Bullets:
• Landing page: capabilities (advance ledger, aging & DSO, debtor credit, real-time alerts,
  same-day funding, portfolio analytics) and live monitoring panels
• Sign in / create account page (/auth) — open a free account or sign in, no card required
• Six consoles, each with its own sidebar, navigation, and permissions:
  1. Factor console (Admin)  2. Treasury desk  3. Checker desk
  4. Operations desk  5. Sales workspace  6. Reporting console (plus Trader portal)
• Cmd+K / Ctrl+K quick-navigation command palette searches every page
• Role walls: each role only sees the pages relevant to its job
Visual: funnel diagram — one login → six role consoles; mini sidebar mock.
Pages covered: Landing page, Sign-in / Sign-up (auth).

────────────────────────────────────────────
SLIDE 3 — DASHBOARD: THE COMMAND CENTER
────────────────────────────────────────────
Headline: "Every number that matters, on one screen"
Bullets:
• Income KPIs: sales (gross), cost of goods, gross income + margin, net income
• Funding KPIs: outstanding receivables (AR), amounts advanced, overdue count, collection rate
• Settlement quality: short payments, average late days, on-time settlements
• Charts: gross vs net income trend (last 8 months), aging waterfall (current / 1-30 / 31-60 /
  61-90 / 90+ days), debtor-concentration vs credit limits (admin)
• Live alerts feed and recent invoices + expenses tables
• Role-aware: factor console shows "Portfolio command", treasury sees "Funding overview"
Visual: screenshot-style dashboard mock with stat tiles, area chart, and waterfall bars.
Pages covered: Dashboard (/app/dashboard).

────────────────────────────────────────────
SLIDE 4 — SALES & INVOICING
────────────────────────────────────────────
Headline: "From sales invoice to funded asset"
Bullets:
• Sales invoices (/app/invoices): submit invoices; each routes to the Checker desk for
  approval before entering the funding queue; statuses paid / overdue / advanced / rejected
• Proforma invoices (/app/proformas): raise a proforma against a PO number to take or release
  an advance; the advance is applied to the final invoice with the same PO
• Credit & debit notes (/app/notes): refunds/discounts (credit) and extra charges/claims
  (debit); every note flows checker → funding queue → treasury applies it to the linked invoice
• Document preview & print pages for invoices and notes, with the company's branded template
Visual: three cards — invoice queue, proforma flow (PO → advance → final invoice), note types.
Pages covered: Sales invoices, Proforma invoices, Credit & Debit notes, Invoice preview,
Note preview.

────────────────────────────────────────────
SLIDE 5 — PROCUREMENT & COSTS
────────────────────────────────────────────
Headline: "Know every dollar in and every dollar out"
Bullets:
• Purchase invoices (/app/purchases): supplier invoices with PO details, linked to the sales
  they support — the cost side of each deal
• Vendors (/app/vendors): the companies you buy from — contacts, payment terms, open payables
• Expenses (/app/expenses): logistics, insurance, interest and other operating costs; link
  each expense to a sales or purchase invoice for true per-deal economics; document attachments
• Advances (/app/advances): money received from customers or paid to suppliers ahead of the
  final invoice; every advance is tied to a specific invoice
Visual: procurement pipeline graphic; expense receipt + chain-link icon showing invoice linking.
Pages covered: Purchase invoices, Vendors, Expenses, Advances.

────────────────────────────────────────────
SLIDE 6 — COUNTERPARTIES & CREDIT RISK
────────────────────────────────────────────
Headline: "Score, limit, and concentration in a single view"
Bullets:
• Debtor book (/app/debtors): credit limits, risk scores, and live exposure across every payer
• Suppliers (/app/suppliers): onboarding for the companies whose invoices you finance — terms,
  credit lines, lifecycle status
• CRM / Salesforce (/app/crm): manage leads, opportunities, and activities with per-user scope
• Trip a credit limit and you'll know before the wire moves — alerts fire immediately
Visual: credit-score gauge, debtor exposure bars vs limits, lead pipeline funnel.
Pages covered: Debtors, Suppliers, CRM (Leads).

────────────────────────────────────────────
SLIDE 7 — APPROVAL, FUNDING & TEAM WORKFLOW
────────────────────────────────────────────
Headline: "Maker–checker control with same-day funding"
Bullets:
• Checker desk (/app/checker): maker–checker review — a second set of eyes approves or rejects
  every submitted transaction before it can be funded
• Funding queue (/app/queue): the Treasury desk — approved invoices and advances awaiting
  disbursement; release reserves on collection
• My Workspace (/app/workspace): submit and track requests — Visits, Travel, Expenses, Leave
• Reporting manager: My Reports (/app/reports) with per-team-member progress, and "view-as"
  to inspect any member's metrics; Team requests (/app/requests) for pending approvals
Visual: two-step approval flow (submit → check → fund) with checkmark and banknote icons;
workspace tab mock.
Pages covered: Checker, Funding queue, My Workspace, My Reports, Team requests.

────────────────────────────────────────────
SLIDE 8 — PRODUCT CATALOG & SKUs  ★ SHOWCASE
────────────────────────────────────────────
Headline: "One master catalog that powers the whole operation"
Subtitle: "Products & SKUs (/app/products) — the single source of truth behind forecasting,
low-stock alerts, and inventory valuation."
Bullets:
• Every SKU with rich attributes: code, name, category (Footwear, Apparel, Accessories,
  Equipment, Nutrition), subcategory, gender, size, color, and season
• Trading data per SKU: unit price, unit cost, reorder level, max stock, supplier lead time,
  and lifecycle status (active / discontinued)
• Live stock-on-hand computed automatically for every product from stock movements — no
  manual counting
• At-a-glance summary tiles: total SKUs, active, low stock, out of stock, and total
  inventory value at cost
• Status pills (Out / Low / OK) that trip the moment stock falls to the reorder level
• Search by SKU / name / color, filter by category, and full create–edit–delete flows
• Reorder levels are the engine inputs: they drive the demand forecast and low-stock alerts
Visual: product catalog table mock with attribute chips, colored Out/Low/OK pills, and the
five stat tiles. Call-out arrow: "reorder level → powers forecast & alerts".
Pages covered: Products & SKUs (/app/products).

────────────────────────────────────────────
SLIDE 9 — INVENTORY: THE STOCK LEDGER  ★ SHOWCASE
────────────────────────────────────────────
Headline: "A double-entry stock ledger, not a spreadsheet"
Subtitle: "Inventory (/app/inventory) — every unit that moves is a dated, valued journal entry."
Bullets:
• Stock-in (credit) from purchase invoices and stock-out (debit) from sales invoices —
  classic double-entry mechanics for physical goods
• Current balances per item: quantity on hand, unit of measure, and live inventory value
• Full movement journal: date, direction, item, quantity, unit cost, and the linked invoice
  (drill straight into the source sales or purchase invoice)
• Smart valuation: stock-in is valued at unit price, stock-out at unit cost
• Bulk entry mode: record many dated movements in one screen with per-row quantities,
  notes, and a running total
• Movements can be linked to catalog products (for forecasting) or recorded as ad-hoc items
  — not every transaction needs inventory
• Filter the journal by stock-in / stock-out; delete mistakes
Visual: T-account ledger mock with green "Credit / Stock-in ↓" and amber "Debit / Stock-out ↑"
arrows, a linked-invoice chip, and the bulk-entry row builder.
Pages covered: Inventory / Stock ledger (/app/inventory).

────────────────────────────────────────────
SLIDE 10 — DEMAND FORECASTING & REORDER INTELLIGENCE  ★ SHOWCASE
────────────────────────────────────────────
Headline: "AI-grade demand forecasting — every SKU, every month"
Subtitle: "Demand forecast & reorder (/app/forecast) — a seasonal weighted-trend model over
12 months of real sales history, recomputed live."
Bullets:
• 12-month seasonal weighted-trend model per SKU: stockout-corrected demand →
  exponentially weighted baseline → weighted trend line → raw seasonal factors →
  business factors (trekking season, weather, promotions, regional demand, events) →
  monthly forecast with 80% confidence intervals
• Reorder math done right: lead-time demand + safety stock (95% service level) − stock on
  hand, capped by max-stock and rounded to MOQ / order multiples
• Actionable timelines per SKU: estimated stockout date, last safe reorder-by date, next
  refill date, and urgency (critical / warning / safe)
• Smart classification: velocity (fast / medium / slow / dead — ranked within each category)
  and momentum (accelerating / stable / declining / inactive)
• Risk flags: stockout risk and overstock risk on every SKU
• Pricing strategy recommendations: clearance, markdown, targeted promotion, hold price,
  protect margin, or protect availability — each with a minimum price floor (40% margin)
• Full transparency: every forecast expands to show its complete calculation breakdown,
  number by number
• Live by design: refreshes every 60 seconds, server-persisted daily snapshots, and a
  one-click recompute; filter by reorder / critical / fast / slow / out
Visual: forecast area chart with shaded 80% prediction-interval band; below it an SKU row with
velocity + momentum badges, a stockout countdown bar, and reorder quantity chip.
Pages covered: Demand forecast & reorder (/app/forecast).

────────────────────────────────────────────
SLIDE 11 — ACCOUNTING & FINANCIAL STATEMENTS
────────────────────────────────────────────
Headline: "Double-entry certainty behind every transaction"
Bullets:
• Accounting (/app/accounting): every financial movement becomes a balanced journal — manage
  the chart of accounts, post manual journals, and drill into every line
• Balance sheet (/app/balance-sheet): as-of-date statement that auto-updates from invoices,
  purchases, inventory, advances, accounts, and manual entries
• Reports & requests stay in sync: reporting managers see team output; approval requests
  surface across the org
• Profile & Settings: manage personal info, photo, and company profile
Visual: T-account / journal-entry diagram on the left, balance-sheet columns on the right.
Pages covered: Accounting, Balance sheet, Profile, Settings.

────────────────────────────────────────────
SLIDE 12 — MONITORING, OPERATIONS & ADMINISTRATION
────────────────────────────────────────────
Headline: "The console doesn't sleep. Neither does your risk."
Bullets:
• Alerts (/app/alerts): real-time surveillance — overdue invoices, credit-limit breaches,
  risk-grade migrations, severity-tagged (info / warning / critical) with unread count
• Reminders (/app/reminders): automated email nudges to keep collections on schedule
• Operations console (/app/admin): generate alerts, manage team roles, and act on exceptions
• Invoice template (/app/template): brand the platform's generated invoices and notes
• Closing message: submit, advance, collect — and watch aging, concentration, and credit risk
  move in real time
Visual: live alert feed with colored severity dots; shield icon; CTA card "Open a free account".
Pages covered: Alerts, Reminders, Operations (admin), Invoice template.
────────────────────────────────────────────
DECK-LEVEL NOTES
• Slides 8–10 are the technical showcase — use richer visuals (charts, ledger mocks,
  formula call-outs) and slightly larger type for the headline.
• Keep one visual per slide, no stock-photo clutter.
• Use consistent blue/cyan accent palette and the "num" tabular style for figures.
• Final slide of the deck = a closing CTA, not a "thank you".
• Speaker-notes hint: mention that approvals (checker) always gate funding (treasury), and
  that the forecast engine's calculation breakdown makes every recommendation auditable.
```
